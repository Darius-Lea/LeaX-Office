## Owner feedback, patrol reliability and private connections

The officer-style office patrol records strikes only for repeated expired execution claims or repeated missing promised reports after recovery. It creates a real CEO alert with the cause and implemented reassignment choices. Normal idle time, source/model setup, approvals, queues and clock-out are exempt. Results lets the owner record actual introductions, replies, quotes, job wins/losses, receipts, paid costs and follow-up dates. Today and the local AI use them to adjust the next step. While clocked in with a selected model, changed results or a saved capability profile queue a new advisory review; missing AI never produces fake advice.
