## Gates before advertising a finished Windows product

1. Execute the actual downloaded file on clean Windows 10/11 x64 devices. Verify install, engine/model inference, app window, network interruption/cancel/resume, update, data/account retention, import/recovery, owner-process stopping and disk/memory failures. Cross-compilation and Linux/browser tests do not replace this.
2. Obtain a legitimate publisher signing certificate and sign the executable. Unknown-publisher/SmartScreen warnings remain possible now. Do not weaken Windows policy or call this signed.
3. Validate live source access and model-assisted workflows with an owner’s private replacement SAM key on the target device. Do not bundle personal credentials. Official current-bid access remains optional; historical awards are not open bids. This is incomplete federal coverage, not all private/global construction, medical or rollout sources.
4. Review commercial distribution terms, privacy/support/account-recovery and backup expectations for the actual customer offering. The existing product is single-founder per local database, not production-audited multi-tenant SaaS. Service-fit screening still targets the APG installation/cleanup profile; arbitrary industries need more work.

Automated fixtures and interface animation do not establish live source/model success, signed publisher status, accepted contracts or earnings. Separately, actual USAspending validation on 2026-10-22 collected and reviewed 60 historical awards with four saved handoffs, three queued contact jobs and one completion learning example in a removed isolated database. This does not establish current bids, company contacts or local-model inference. Pricing is owner planning only; confirm scope, taxes/fees, delivery cost, qualifications and payment terms before offering a quote.
