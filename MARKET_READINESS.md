# Release status: 0.2.0-preview.5

The current build is a single downloadable Windows x64 setup executable with automatic local AI. It is not yet a validated commercial release.

## Delivered

- Public `/download` landing page and complete executable download; no source ZIP, sign-in or file browsing required to obtain setup. Private owner APIs/backups remain authenticated.
- Production bundle/minification, local vendor assets and integrity manifest. Original source folders, development documents, tests, databases, personal keys and source maps are excluded from the customer payload. Runtime npm is removed. Executable/browser code can still be inspected; this is not copy protection.
- Automatic verified Node/private Ollama/model setup, real inference prerequisite, saved connection, progress/cancel/retry, private data directory, current-version reuse and staged updates. Standalone app-style window through a trusted installed browser, with normal-browser fallback. No paid services or startup task installed.
- Screen-filling detailed 3D office with drag/pinch/orbit, accessible lightweight views, owner desktop and real saved-report/blocked-work notifications. Manager and delivery movements are tied to recorded checks/handoffs.
- Source-backed next actions, explicit research/contact/draft pipeline, owner readiness confirmations, company customization and persistent USD cost/margin worksheets. No fabricated clients, sent mail, contracts, invoices or revenue.
- Printable assignment packets and backup/transfer recovery. Completed Bravo cycles remain available as packets.
- Automatic bounded manager planning and contact follow-through, with durable directives, source cooldowns, daily limits and clock-out protection. CEO reminders are not required to assign the next eligible job.
- Live operations side monitor with real employee actions, work receipts, manager plan and saved business metrics. Reward/correct controls train a persisted Bayesian job-priority model from eligible receipts; they do not fine-tune the language model or penalize normal waiting.
- Richer procedural office materials, furniture, lighting, people and actual-status monitor displays. No measured tenfold realism or photorealistic rendering claim.

## Gates before advertising a finished Windows product

1. Execute the actual downloaded file on clean Windows 10/11 x64 devices. Verify install, engine/model inference, app window, network interruption/cancel/resume, update, data/account retention, import/recovery, owner-process stopping and disk/memory failures. Cross-compilation and Linux/browser tests do not replace this.
2. Obtain a legitimate publisher signing certificate and sign the executable. Unknown-publisher/SmartScreen warnings remain possible now. Do not weaken Windows policy or call this signed.
3. Validate live source access and model-assisted workflows with an owner’s private replacement SAM key on the target device. Do not bundle personal credentials. Official current-bid access remains optional; historical awards are not open bids. This is incomplete federal coverage, not all private/global construction, medical or rollout sources.
4. Review commercial distribution terms, privacy/support/account-recovery and backup expectations for the actual customer offering. The existing product is single-founder per local database, not production-audited multi-tenant SaaS. Service-fit screening still targets the APG installation/cleanup profile; arbitrary industries need more work.

Automated fixtures and interface animation do not establish live source/model success, signed publisher status, accepted contracts or earnings. Separately, actual USAspending validation on 2026-10-22 collected and reviewed 60 historical awards with four saved handoffs, three queued contact jobs and one completion learning example in a removed isolated database. This does not establish current bids, company contacts or local-model inference. Pricing is owner planning only; confirm scope, taxes/fees, delivery cost, qualifications and payment terms before offering a quote.
