# AI Headquarters operating model

The owner's objective is profitable, deliverable work for American Power Global, not a busy-looking office. Apply the controls of a disciplined service company: named ownership, evidence, explicit handoffs, measurable results, exceptions and human approval. The current application is an early local/hosted research workflow; it is not yet a complete enterprise operating system.

## Manager patrol contract

While the office is clocked in and the host is awake, Johnson checks at a bounded interval, not on an endless busy loop. Inspect one employee in the office view as part of the real patrol record; present the check time and next check. At minimum:

- Recover an expired research lease for another bounded attempt when retry allowance remains. Preserve checkpoints and history. If the allowance is exhausted, block and escalate; do not declare success.
- Prioritize eligible existing tasks, handoffs and contact follow-through. Update the company register from actual contact receipts, prepare source-backed unsent drafts and deliver a concise contact update. Select another permitted research service lane when these dependencies allow it; the CEO need not continually issue start instructions.
- Find blocked assignments, retain the actual error, and tell the owner the concrete prerequisite or next action. Do not retry an unchanged network failure continuously.
- Check that completed research has its four follow-up jobs and saved outputs. Reconcile missing queued handoffs without duplicating completed work or owner notifications.
- Distinguish scheduled waiting, useful running work, missing tools, source failure and owner approval. An empty queue between research cycles is not employee misconduct.
- Deliver a new attention note when the issue set changes. Keep repeated unchanged problems visible without producing a fresh notification every minute.

Clock-out stops new work. An active read-only page may finish safely. Clock-in permission can persist across tab closure and process restart, but it cannot keep a free Render host awake or make an ephemeral database durable.

Automatic award planning permits up to 24 cycles per rolling day, at least 20 minutes apart and six hours between the same lane. Each award cycle retains its three-page/60-record bound. Failed source requests back off from one hour to at most six hours. Contact starts are limited to 12 confirmed-site and four discovery attempts per rolling day; search-index failure backs off six hours while confirmed-site work remains eligible. Follow-through permits six unsent drafts and 12 contact updates per rolling day. At 20 pending contact jobs, more award collection pauses so the backlog can progress. These are ceilings, not promises of that many useful leads.

## Live supervision and learning

The Live operations side monitor presents saved employee actions, the manager's directives and next check, actual work receipts and business statistics. Only an executing saved job is Working; queued work, monitoring, setup, source waiting, approvals and off duty have separate states. The manager/security patrol does not manufacture a slacker to animate or punish.

The owner can inspect an eligible saved receipt, reward useful work, or record a correction and reason. A persisted Bayesian Beta-Bernoulli bandit updates work-recipe preferences; ranking applies only after clock, tool, source, budget and cooldown gates. Validated execution receipts provide a small automatic signal; owner feedback carries greater weight. A correction asks Johnson to arrange an eligible recovery or a genuine local-model quality review. Missing tools leave that review waiting, without inventing a completed correction. Original outputs remain intact.

This is online learning of work priorities, not language-model fine-tuning or a claim that avatars understand punishment. Deduplication prevents repeated clicks from multiplying the same training signal. Excusing an incident removes it from active failure training while keeping the audit history. Normal waiting, setup, source outages and approval dependencies never count as misconduct.

## Revenue workflow and acceptance gates

1. **Discover:** source-linked historical recipients or current opportunities. Label which type each record is. Record freshness and service/geographic relevance.
2. **Verify and qualify:** verify the company, current vendor program or project, public business contact source, APG service fit and delivery constraints. Unknown information stays unknown.
3. **Prepare outreach:** select a legitimate recipient, review contact history and opt-outs, write a relevant draft, and obtain explicit approval within permitted limits.
4. **Manage response:** record an actual reply; classify it; schedule an authorized follow-up or appointment. Never infer interest from a successful send.
5. **Estimate and propose:** calculate labor, materials, travel, subcontractor cost, risk, margin and terms from verified inputs. An approved proposal is not an accepted contract.
6. **Confirm and dispatch:** require actual customer authorization plus confirmed scope, staffing, qualifications, dates and economics. A researched company is not a booked job.
7. **Deliver and collect:** record completion evidence, customer acceptance, invoice, due date and received payment. Forecast value and collected revenue are separate records.

Historical discovery, bounded public contact research, administrative handoffs, unsent introductions, advisory qualification and saved pricing drafts currently execute. A live USAspending run reviewed 60 historical records and saved its handoffs; it did not prove current demand or contact availability. Live keyed current-notice validation, reliable company/decision-maker verification, authorized email/reply handling, accepted proposals, real dispatch and invoicing/payment workflows remain incomplete. The manager exposes these gaps rather than generating unsupported results.

## Owner supervision and scorecard

The owner computer should show an actionable next step, original evidence, accountable employee, unresolved dependency and latest review time. Owner corrections belong alongside source evidence, not over it. Retain an audit record for edits, reassignments, recovery and external actions.

Measure verified outputs: new relevant companies, current opportunities, source-verified business contacts, qualification decisions, approved messages actually sent, replies, requested proposals, confirmed work, gross margin and collected revenue. Show unknown or zero honestly. Do not use avatar movement, note volume, total award value or generic drafts as a revenue-performance metric.
