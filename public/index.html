YOUR WORKSPACE, YOUR DEVICE

Settings & installation

1 · Run locally and keep your data

On Windows 10/11 (64-bit), download and open AI-Headquarters-Setup.exe. Setup starts automatically. A native setup window installs the verified runtime and opens your local office without a PowerShell script or terminal. Your data stays in your Windows user folder across updates.

Download Windows setup (.exe)

This native installer is currently unsigned and Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning. Actual installation on Windows still needs device validation. Keep the computer awake for background work. No software can work while it is powered off.

2 · Built-in local AI — automatic

The latest Windows download installs its own private AI engine, chooses a starter model for your computer and tests the connection automatically. You do not install Ollama separately, choose a model or enter an AI API key. First setup downloads roughly 2–5 GB and needs a computer with at least 4 GB RAM and 12 GB free disk space. Model quality and speed depend on your hardware.

Device details

If your existing local app predates automatic AI setup, download and open the latest Windows setup to upgrade. Saved business data stays separate.

Advanced model controls (optional)

These controls are optional. The installer normally handles model installation and selection. A small fallback model uses roughly 0.5–0.7 GB; larger models need more memory.

Optional · Current federal bid notices

Historical award research works without a SAM key. Current federal opportunities use SAM.gov’s official API and require your free account’s key. Official free-account and key instructions. In the Windows launcher, use the optional masked SAM key field; it is protected for your Windows user. If staying on Render, add SAM_API_KEY in Render Environment settings. Never paste the key in chat. Reopen the local office after saving a key.

No key means Setup required, not a list of invented open jobs.

4 · Choose your direction

Save your company name, services and standing objective under company files. Opening the signed-in app starts work unless you explicitly clock out. Closing the tab does not erase work or clock out. Keep private backups of the data folder.
