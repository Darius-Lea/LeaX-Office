Before you install

First setup downloads about 2–5 GB. Allow at least 12 GB of free storage and 4 GB RAM. Keep your computer awake for background work. Performance varies by hardware.

This preview is unsigned and has not completed Windows-device release validation. Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning. Only use the download from your app owner’s site.

AI suggestions need review. Historical awards are not open bids. Optional current federal opportunity searches require your own free SAM.gov key. No email is automatically sent and no income is promised.

Your business records are stored separately on your computer. Keep private backups. Existing hosted records require an explicit backup transfer.
