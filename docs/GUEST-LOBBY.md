# Passwordless guest lobby — preview.46

Sign in through `/offices`, then choose **Guest lobby invitations**. Owners and co-owners choose an office and an expiration (5–240 minutes) at `/guest`, create a link and send it privately to one guest. The guest opens it on desktop or phone, enters a display name, and joins without creating an account. Link secrets are in URL fragments, removed before subsequent navigation, and stored as hashes on the server. A separate HttpOnly SameSite cookie carries the guest session; it does not authenticate shared-account or founder APIs.

The management screen shows whether a link is waiting, used, joined, or revoked, and the guest's display name. Its list refreshes every five seconds. **Revoke access** denies the next guest request immediately; the guest screen checks every three seconds and closes its lobby view. Expiration, loss of the link creator's owner/co-owner membership and creator account suspension also deny guest access. Links are one-use, and both the invitation and resulting guest session expire at the selected time. Leaving consumes the session; a new invitation is needed to rejoin.

For web/desktop sharing, both must use the same updated HTTPS service with durable storage. Installed offices configured with an authoritative access origin generate links to that origin; an unconfigured localhost server cannot invite another device. Creating the link does not send email or messages automatically.

Preview.47 adds **two-person guest video/audio meetings in the lobby**. After joining, the guest presses Request video meeting. The host presses Answer meeting or Decline meeting on the matching invitation. Each person explicitly grants their own microphone/camera permission; camera is optional. Controls include independent microphone mute, camera off/on, hang-up, remote video playback and fullscreen (where the browser supports it). No call is recorded by this application. Keep the page open. Revocation, guest departure, expiry, loss of host authority, account holds, or failed authorization/network polling stops local capture and peer media. A missing heartbeat ends the server-side call after 30 seconds; ringing requests time out after 60 seconds.

## Office walkthroughs and guided 3D views

An owner or co-owner can invite a guest to the existing office, choose the destination office, and share the one-use link. After joining, the guest can explore independently or choose **Follow host's tour**. Following mirrors the host's position and camera inside the office scene; the guest can leave the tour and move independently at any time. The host can separately choose **Follow guest view**, which mirrors the guest's 3D position and camera and places a host avatar behind the guest in the scene. This does not share the guest's browser tabs or desktop. Screen sharing is a separate, optional browser permission.

The guest panel explains what the host can see. Both directions stop when the tour ends, the guest leaves, the invite expires or is revoked, or access checks fail. Office controls fit touch screens; the Monetization campus lowers render resolution and animation rate on smaller or lower-power devices. The existing lobby file remains unchanged; the office pages reuse its blue glass, cyan glow and motion treatment.

Video/audio meetings remain a separate explicit flow. Each participant grants their own microphone/camera access, and guest screen sharing requires an additional browser prompt. The application does not record the call. Public Render deployment, a production TURN relay, physical phones and external-network walkthroughs are not verified by the local source checks. The Google Sites embed issue remains unresolved.

## Relay configuration

WebRTC media uses DTLS-SRTP between participants, directly or through the configured relay. Signaling requires the same HTTPS service used by accounts. STUN defaults to `stun:stun.l.google.com:19302`; override with `HQ_STUN_URL` (set `none` to disable). STUN alone cannot connect every network. Configure your own existing TURN relay for restrictive networks: `HQ_TURN_URL` accepts up to four comma-separated `turn:`/`turns:` URLs. Prefer `HQ_TURN_SECRET` matching the relay’s REST shared secret: participants receive generated credentials expiring no later than their invitation or one hour. For existing static-credential relays, use `HQ_TURN_USER` and `HQ_TURN_PASSWORD` instead. Never put the shared secret in client code. No production relay is provisioned or paid for by this change.

Only the guest session for that invitation and the answering host account session can exchange call signals. Other guests and other host browser sessions cannot inspect a connected call. Polling uses cursors so retried reads do not lose negotiation messages. Pending media signals are bounded and expire after two minutes; ending the call deletes them. Expired invitation records remove their call records during maintenance. Calls are not available offline.
